Lesson 5 · 15 min

Task contract for a browser agent (goal, limits, proof, stop)

"Book a service for me on the site" sounds like a request. For a browser agent, everything is missing: which page, what it can touch, how to prove it is done, and when to stop. In this lesson you write a task contract in four parts and check with a script that the contract is complete before handing it to any agent.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Goal: where and what
  2. 2Limits: what it can and can't do
  3. 3Proof: the text you see on the page
  4. 4Stop: when to stop and call the person

Goal: write the contract for a browser task with goal, limits, proof and stop, and check that it is complete.

01 · The four parts

Tap each block. Read from bottom to top when you review.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Without proof, the result is a promise. Without limits, the whole site is at stake. Without a stop, the agent keeps pushing until it makes things up.

02 · Writing each part

What conferir-contrato.mjs checks for, and why.

1. Goal

20 letters or more and a place: an address or a page name. "On the shop's site" won't do; "on the agendar.html page" will.

One task, one result. Two things become two contracts.

2. Limits

The script requires at least one "no": the agent needs to know what not to do. In a site test, almost always: don't confirm, don't pay, don't leave the address.

Data limits count too: "don't type a password", "don't use someone else's data".

3. Proof

A literal text, in quotes, that you can search for on the page. It's the same text the dry-run robot in lesson 6 will wait for.

Also ask for the evidence copied from the page: "return the times copied from the list, not from memory".

4. Stop

The signals (login, verification, confirmation) and a number: 10 actions, 3 attempts. The script requires a number.

Ask for a well-reported blocker: where it stopped, what's missing, and the action the person needs to take.

Well-reported blocker"I stopped at the account login screen of conta.html: it asks for email and password. Missing: the account owner's login. Nothing was sent." Better than "I couldn't."

03 · A complete contract and a vague one

The same script checks both. The part titles also work in English and Spanish (Goal, Limits, Proof, Stop; Objetivo, Límites, Prueba, Parada).

File · kit/contratos/exemplo.md
# Contrato: horários de troca de pneu

## Objetivo
Na página http://127.0.0.1:8433/agendar.html, descobrir os horários livres de "Troca de pneu" em 2026-11-03.

## Limites
Pode preencher o formulário e clicar em "Ver horários". Não clique em "Agendar às…" nem em "Confirmar". Não saia de http://127.0.0.1:8433/.

## Prova
A página mostra "4 horários livres para Troca de pneu em 2026-11-03." Devolva os horários copiados da lista, não de memória.

## Parada
Pare e descreva a tela se aparecer login, verificação humana ou pedido de confirmação. Pare depois de 10 ações sem chegar à prova.

"Schedule a service for me on the shop's website" doesn't name the page, has no limits, no proof, and no stop. An agent with that request might click Confirmar.

04 · Practice

From vague to complete, and a contract for your site. Each exercise has a "done when".

E1 · Fix the vague one

10 min

Copy kit/contratos/vago.md to meu-contrato.md and rewrite it until the script accepts it. Use the conta.html page: the proof is the list of appointments, and the stop is the login screen.

Done when: node conferir-contrato.mjs meu-contrato.md → contrato ok: 4/4 partes and saída: 0.

E2 · A task from your own site

15 min

Write the contract for a real task on your site (local or staging), read-only. The proof must be text you can find on the page right now.

Done when: node conferir-contrato.mjs contrato-site.md → contrato ok: 4/4 partes, and the proof text shows up with Ctrl+F on the page.

06 · In three sentences

Four partsGoal with a place, limits with a "no", proof in quotes, stop with a number.
Proof is not a sentenceIt's text you look for on the page and find.
Stopping is a resultStopping with the exact reason is worth more than pushing on and making things up.
